What does sign in mean?
Sign in to sync your progress

Signing in is optional. It lets your learned words and bookmarks follow you across phones, tablets, and computers.

Google securely handles your sign-in. Vocab 333 never receives or stores your Google password. We save only your study progress and basic account identification needed to keep each user’s data separate.

If you continue without signing in, the app still works normally, but your progress stays in this browser on this device. You can sign out at any time.
